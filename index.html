Gear I Love

Two machines built for going fast

One for the road and one for the mountain. These are two of my favorite pieces of design.

Porsche 911 Turbo S

The 911 has kept the same basic shape for more than sixty years, with round headlights, a sloping roof and the engine in the back. The latest Turbo S uses a hybrid system to put out more power than any 911 before it.

  • 0–60 mph2.4 s
  • Top speed200 mph
  • EngineTwin-turbo flat-6 hybrid

Faction Studio 2

The Studio 2 is a freestyle ski for park and all-mountain riding. It has twin tips for skiing switch, a light poplar core and a medium flex, so it feels playful without getting unstable at speed. The graphics stand out on any lift.

  • Waist96 mm
  • ShapeTwin tip
  • Best forPark & all-mountain
